WHY 303 s AND NOT THE RECORDED 187 s
----------------------------------------------------------------
The Gazebo GUI was rendering. RETURN_HOME took 161.7 s against the 81.0 s
of the headless release run, and Nav2 ran a spin and a backup on the way;
every other state is within a few seconds of the headless timings. The
run is a functional check of the documented commands, not a timing
measurement — the measured nominal stays 186.7 s, headless, in
release_nominal_mission.txt.
