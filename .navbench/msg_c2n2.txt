C2-NAV.2: the BaseObstacle scale is not the stall, and cannot be

One variable, and the last surviving hypothesis for the enclosure_entry
stall. FollowPath.BaseObstacle.scale, 8.0 -> 2.0. No source file, launch
file or test was touched.

The baseline is C2-NAV.0, NOT C2-NAV.1. The worktree carried C2-NAV.1's
PositionGoalChecker, which would have made this two variables, so
nav2_params.yaml was restored from 8f05c45 first and then edited in one
place. Verified two ways: a comment-stripped diff against 8f05c45 reduces
to -BaseObstacle.scale: 8.0 / +BaseObstacle.scale: 2.0 and nothing else;
and the LIVE controller_server reports BaseObstacle.scale 2.0, sum_scores
False, goal checker nav2_controller::SimpleGoalChecker at 0.25/0.25, with
PathAlign/PathDist 32.0, GoalAlign/GoalDist 24.0, RotateToGoal 32.0,
vx_samples 20, vtheta_samples 40, sim_time 1.5, controller_frequency 10.0,
robot_radius 0.20, inflation_radius 0.50 and slowdown_ratio 0.3 unchanged.

The params file must be passed explicitly. install/gazebo_models symlinks
its config/ to the trunk, which is at main and still holds 8.0; without
params_file:=<worktree>/... this experiment silently re-runs the baseline
and reports it as the result.

REJECTED. 3 repeats, topology A, 75 s timeout, fresh headless sim, robot
verified at the spawn, RTF 0.991. enclosure_entry 0/3 before and 0/3
after, and worse on every movement metric: longest stall 47.84 -> 64.21 s,
distance remaining at the stall 1.150 -> 1.322 m, DWB best vx == 0 on
0.680 -> 0.921 of cycles, best vx mean 0.048 -> 0.004.

Not a null result. The intervention worked on the quantity it targeted:
BaseObstacle's share of the chosen trajectory's score fell 71.8 % -> 0.0 %.
The named mechanism was removed and the symptom did not move.

Measured deeper than success/failure, off DWB's own /evaluation. Two stall
poses, two different reasons. At 1.313 m out the robot sits in a cost-0
cell with a 1.90 m zero-cost band, chooses vx 0.0 in 12 of 12 cycles, and
8 of 10 sampled forward speeds are scored TO COMPLETION with BaseObstacle
= 0.00 and still lose, median gap 7.90, carried by PathAlign +34.40,
GoalAlign +29.40, GoalDist +18.00, PathDist +14.40. So BaseObstacle is not
even a necessary condition. At 1.271 m out, 0.165 m deeper, all 10 forward
speeds abort on BaseObstacle alone, 120.0-262.0 against a winning total of
34.0 -- cell costs of 60-131.

And that closes the knob. With sum_scores false the MapGrid critics'
effective weight is resolution*0.5*scale = 0.60 per cell, so the winning
zero-velocity total is only ~33 and forward motion is disqualified once
cost*scale exceeds it: cost ~17 at scale 2.0, ~4 at scale 8.0. The pinch
presents 60-131, needing scale < 0.26-0.57 -- below the 0.02 C2-NAV.0
forbade returning to. The scale cannot reach the behaviour without
recreating the defect it was raised to fix.

The falsifier was already committed: C2-NAV.0 repeat 2 stalled 48.21 s
with BaseObstacle at 0.0 % of the chosen score. The 93.4 % was one instant
in one repeat, never the population.

The robot is rotating, not frozen: 5.550 rad over the 64.21 s stall,
commanded w reaching max_vel_theta 1.0 rad/s against an actual median of
0.027 rad/s, /cmd_vel_nav linear zero on 96.7 % of samples, collision
monitor SLOWDOWN 75.3 % / DO_NOTHING 16.3 %. The progress checker aborts
follow_path every 10 s because a rotating robot never translates 0.1 m.

Two honesty notes. The baseline ran enclosure_entry as leg 6 of a 7-leg
tour; this ran --only enclosure_entry, so only repeat 0 is a fresh
approach and repeats 1 and 2 start where repeat 0 stalled and are escape
tests. Their 0.195 m and 0.494 m driven distances must not be compared
with the baseline's 3.3-4.0 m; the comparable number is repeat 0's 1.320 m
goal error against the baseline's 1.159-1.449 m, inside that range. And
the first pass of the critic decomposition was wrong: short_circuit_
trajectory_evaluation is true, so an aborted trajectory carries only the
critics scored before the abort and its total is partial; differencing it
as if the missing critics were 0.0 manufactured a spurious -195 for
GoalAlign. c2n2_reanalyse.py separates the two cases and every number
above comes from the corrected pass.

BaseObstacle.scale: 2.0 IS NOT APPROVED and must not be merged. It is left
in the file, commented with its own verdict, only as the record. Four of
the four original candidate causes of enclosure_entry are now eliminated
by measurement and the stall is still unexplained; C2-NAV.3 is scoped in
docs/ROADMAP.md as a DIAGNOSIS of the goal/path MapGrid, not a tuning step.

Infrastructure, recorded and deliberately NOT fixed here: ros_clean.sh
brackets every pkill pattern except 'nav2_', so any helper whose name
contains that substring is killed by the sweep it invokes. c2nav2_up.sh
matched and the first run died at exit 144 before the simulator started.
All C2-NAV.2 artefacts are named c2n2_* for that reason; bracketing it to
'nav[2]_' is a one-character fix that belongs in its own commit.

gazebo_models 41/41 pass, from inside the package dir on a clean graph
with --ignore=test/test_integration. No other package runs: the change is
a Nav2 YAML value no test reads.
